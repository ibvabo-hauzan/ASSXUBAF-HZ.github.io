ASS X UBAF

Welcome to the websites of two clubs combined, ASS and UBAF. These two clubs are here to remove boredom entirely in the weekends and because of that, we collaborated. The rules are simple, if you are bored and unemployed, you can join this. we will select you if you are perfect. There are many things we can do with these two clubs but first im going to explain both ASS and UBAF. Our clubs have their own history and we will explain those too.

ASS

Autisticly Stupid Specialist

If you're bored and have lots of spare time while your friends have the same problem? This club is made so we can have fun in many ways. Mainly these activity below is what we usually do.

Game On Often Night (GOON)

The game could be anything(mainly our own creation) but in most cases, we GOON in broad daylight

Dinner In Cold Kitchen (DICK)

We sometimes eat noodles once a week, but we also sometimes eat snacks while we GOON.

Discussion At Many Nooks (DAMN)

We discuss anything. From games to random shit, and devices to actual shit. Best combined with DICK.

UBAF

Unemployed and Bored As F#$k

If you're bored and have lots of spare time but you are alone? This club is made so you can have fun without the needs of your friends. Mainly these activity below is what we usually do.

Hint and Sprint

If someone is trying to find someone else. You can try finding it yourself. Although, if you find them, you can't say anything to the person seeking him.

Create and Relate

You can try creating something in your mind at anytime. Even better is when you make it for GOON.
